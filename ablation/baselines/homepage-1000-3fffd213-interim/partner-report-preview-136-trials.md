# Infobric homepage (infobric.com/se) — persona study pilot, page report

*136 simulated visitors (personas) · 2026-09-19 · run `pg-web-infobric-audit-v2-homepage-3fffd213` · model gpt-5.6-luna · instrument v2.2*

**How to read this report.** Each statement carries its evidence layer: **[site]** visible on the website · **[analytics]** supported by the supplied 90-day analytics · **[persona]** modelled persona interpretation. Persona scores are indicators for this pilot, not measured customer approval or conversion forecasts. Page wording is quoted exactly as written, in Swedish. Counts from the visitors' structured answers are exact; counts inside the theme tables were clustered by the model from the visitors' free-text notes and are approximate.

## Summary

- [persona] The homepage makes the offer understandable and relevant: about 91 of 136 visitors found “Vi samlar hanteringen av personal, maskiner och fordon i ett och samma ekosystem.” credible, while about 107 of 136 found “Se när, var och hur era maskiner och verktyg används.” familiar.
- [persona] The main conversion barrier is not basic understanding but purchase readiness: all 136 of 136 visitors said price, integrations, setup time and references were missing, and about 128 of 136 needed references before taking action.
- [persona] Visitors valued the offer but were often not ready to contact sales; about 60 of 136 planned to come back later, 28 of 136 wanted to learn more, and only 48 of 136 chose contact sales, with comparable customer evidence and practical implementation information needed first.
- [analytics] The page attracts substantial engagement, with about 17,190 of 19,442 page sessions engaged, but the 43.76% quick-back rate indicates a substantial expectation, relevance or navigation mismatch; no page-level key events were attributed to the page.
- [site] The page should retain its construction focus, ecosystem framing and concrete solution language, but replace unsupported breadth and leadership claims such as “marknadens bredaste utbud” with comparable cases, workflows, implementation information and clearer next steps.

## 9. Method, coverage and limits

- **Personas:** 136 synthetic visitors drawn proportionally from the six visitor kinds in the brief (§4) with Swedish construction-sector roles, each carrying its own reason for visiting (a specific problem today, or exploring) and prior familiarity with Infobric. They were not shown the analytics.
- **Visit:** each persona briefed itself, opened the page in a real browser, judged the first screen (with leaving as a valid outcome), read the whole page, clicked the primary button once, then answered the questionnaire and rated the six dimensions on the anchors above.
- **Coverage:** 145 of 1000 visits completed, 136 passed the automated checks (94%); persona fidelity 100%; 0.97 of quoted phrases were found verbatim on the page (grounding check).
- **Two readings of every score:** in-browse ratings cluster on the polite middle for some dimensions (a known effect of rating at the end of a long browsing session); the scored-after-the-visit reading has the same persona re-rate from its own notes. Where both agree, the result is robust; where they differ, §1 shows both.
