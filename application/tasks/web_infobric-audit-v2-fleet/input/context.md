# The page you are visiting

A product page on Infobric's Swedish site for Infobric Fleet, a system for managing
company vehicles, drivers, driving logs, vehicle costs, maintenance and reporting. It is
written for Swedish organisations that operate or manage vehicles — of any kind, not only
construction.

You may know little or nothing about Infobric or this product. Read the page as
yourself: someone with your job, your responsibilities and your reasons for looking today.
