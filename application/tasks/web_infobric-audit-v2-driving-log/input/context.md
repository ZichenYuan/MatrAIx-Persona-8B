# The page you are visiting

A product page on Infobric's Swedish site for its electronic driving log (elektronisk
körjournal): a device and app that record business and private journeys for company
cars, service vehicles and pool cars, for mileage reimbursement, tax documentation and
toll handling. It is written for Swedish organisations that run such vehicles.

You may know little or nothing about Infobric or this product. Read the page as
yourself: someone with your job, your responsibilities and your reasons for looking today.
