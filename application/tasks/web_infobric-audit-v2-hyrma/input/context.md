# The page you are visiting

A product page on Infobric's Swedish site for Infobric Hyrma, a rental management
platform for companies that rent out machines, equipment and related products: bookings,
contracts, inventory, transport, returns, service and invoicing in one system. It is
written for Swedish rental businesses.

You may know little or nothing about Infobric or this product. Read the page as
yourself: someone with your job, your responsibilities and your reasons for looking today.
