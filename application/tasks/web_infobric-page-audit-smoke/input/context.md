# Your situation

You work in the Swedish construction industry. Your company is looking at
**Infobric**, a supplier of systems for construction sites — site access and
attendance registers (personalliggare), safety and quality checklists, and
tracking of machines, tools and vehicles.

Find the situation below that matches **your Tier**, and read the page with that
situation in mind. You have limited time and a real decision to make.

## If your Tier is "Main contractor"

You run sites for a main contractor. From next month you take over a project
with around 40 people on site from a dozen different subcontractors. Swedish law
requires you to keep an electronic attendance register, and your client audits
it. Your current setup is a paper list plus three separate systems for machines,
vehicles and safety rounds, and it is not holding up. Two other suppliers have already been
suggested to you, and your current systems do technically work. You have been asked
to come back this week with a recommendation.

## If your Tier is "Subcontractor"

You run a small firm — a handful of people, you do the admin yourself in the
evenings. You have just won work on an NCC site starting in three weeks. The main
contractor requires your people to register on their system, and you also need to
know where your own tools and van are. You cannot afford a long implementation or
a system that needs someone to manage it. The simplest option is to do only what the main
contractor requires and nothing more. You are checking whether this supplier is
worth more than that.

## If your Tier is "Developer"

You are on the client side, commissioning projects. You are accountable for what
happens on sites you pay for: who was there, whether safety rounds were done, and
whether you can produce an audit trail if something goes wrong. You do not run
the sites yourself — your contractors do — so anything you buy has to work across
several contractors at once. You are reviewing several suppliers before the next
procurement round and are early in that process — nothing is decided.

## If your Tier is "Rental"

You rent out machines and tools. Your problem is knowing where your equipment
actually is, what is on hire, what is idle, and getting it all onto invoices
without chasing people. Items go missing between sites and you write them off.
You have looked at tracking products before and found them
expensive for what they do. You are looking for something that tracks equipment and
connects to how you handle rentals and billing.
