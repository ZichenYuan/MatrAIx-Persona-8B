# The page you are visiting

A product page on Infobric's Swedish site for Infobric Equipment, a system for keeping
track of tools and equipment: what exists, where it is, who has it, and service and
calibration. It is written for Swedish organisations that own, rent or manage tools and
equipment across sites, depots or teams.

You may know little or nothing about Infobric or this product. Read the page as
yourself: someone with your job, your responsibilities and your reasons for looking today.
